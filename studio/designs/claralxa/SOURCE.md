# Invitation reference

https://invitato.net/template-claralxa/?code=DUMMY-P0001

This reference uses individual artwork motion rather than a video. Flowers keep swaying on a five-second cycle (0°, 4°, -2°); petals rotate (0°, 30°, -15°). Additive transforms retain custom positions and uploaded artwork. Cinematic off and reduced motion stop the continuous effects. Existing text, photo, music and animation editing remain available.
