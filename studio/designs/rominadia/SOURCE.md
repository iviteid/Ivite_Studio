# Invitation reference

https://invitato.net/template-rominadia/?code=DUMMY-P0001

Opening scenery uses the reference’s 15-second original video, remuxed without audio, looping continuously. Editable names and content remain separate. Motion-off and playback failure use a still poster. Standalone HTML embeds the video and poster.

The opening now runs once. Subsequent cycles start at 9 seconds, within the settled scenery. The tail plays forward and back to avoid restarting the entrance or a visible jump; Aurélie’s exit/fade is excluded.
